# Welcome to the Paddock

A Formula 1 fanfic I've been slowly writing on Wattpad — still very much in progress.

## The TL;DR

Dan Wolfersson was done — three months off-grid, sipping shakes on his yacht, dreaming of a quiet store in the suburbs. Then his dad died, and his sister's trembling voice pulled him back to London.

Now he's stuck as CEO of Wolfersson F1, a team his father bought on a whim — a broke, scandal-ridden mess with no drivers and a staff that won't even wave back. Kate says it was Dad's last wish, but all Dan hears is "selfish prick" ringing in his ears.

The newspapers call him a karting has-been, the board wants to sell, and every F1 legend he chases — Lando, Lewis, Vettel — slips through his fingers. He's got one shot to prove he's not a failure, to honor the old man he barely knew.

Welcome to the paddock, Dan.
